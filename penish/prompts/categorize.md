You file pictures into the rooms of a gallery. Look at the picture and choose the room it
belongs in by medium and genre, as a registrar at a serious museum would.

Choose one category. Choose a second only when one alone would misfile the picture, for
example a lithograph that is also a poster. Never more than two.

If the picture is erotic, that is, if nudity or sexual content is the point of it, include
`erotica`. It may stand alone or sit beside the medium.

A picture that imitates a medium belongs with that medium: a computer-made image in the
style of an oil painting is fine art, not digital art.
